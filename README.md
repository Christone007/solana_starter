**REFLECTION**

PROBLEMS
1. Swapping NFTs this way could lead to losses because one party could send NFT and get nothing in return from the other party.
2. Swapping NFTs would require building a lot of trust between sender and receiver and that defeats the entire essence of blockchain and decentralization.
3. Considering that blockchains have no KYC requirements, lost assets are forever lost and cannot be recovered using law enforcement
4. Trust is hard to build because network participants are usually anonymous
5. NFTs cannot be sent to random people who are not guaranteed to send something in return

SOLUTIONS
1. An automated middle-man that receives NFTs from the 2 parties who have agreed to swap and then sends the NFTs across.
2. Neither party must have control over the middle-man.
3. The middle-man should not be controlled by any party that can influence the transaction.
